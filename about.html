THE RAVENIX
STORY.

“Five very different people finding one place where they finally belong.”

RAVENIX

RAVENIX (레이브닉스) is a five-member South Korean boy group built around the idea of five very different people finding one place where they finally belong.

Before becoming RAVENIX, Kael, Zayn, Nox, Elior, and Rayden weren't originally meant to debut together. They entered the company at different times, trained for different projects, and had completely different strengths.

Some had already watched friends debut while they remained trainees. Others had been moved between potential lineups so many times that debut started to feel impossible.
